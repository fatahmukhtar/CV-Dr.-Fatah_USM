Ir. Ts. Dr. Muhamed Abdul Fatah Muhamed Mukhtar: building reliable solder joints for advanced electronic packaging.

I combine numerical simulation, experiments and machine learning to understand how solder joints, capacitors and moulding compounds behave in electronic assemblies. Before joining Universiti Sains Malaysia I worked as a process engineer at Western Digital, a senior consultant at SIRIM QAS and a lecturer at DRB-HICOM University. Based at the Engineering Campus, Nibong Tebal, Penang.
